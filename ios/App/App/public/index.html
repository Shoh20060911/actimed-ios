Your Health. Our Priority.

An interactive, high-fidelity prototype of a diabetes and health monitoring app that combines the Actimed Band wristband and the Actimed Glucose Sensor into one dashboard, with medicines, alerts, Senior Mode and caregiver sharing.

Prototype only. Every reading is simulated on this page. It is not a medical device and cannot diagnose or detect emergencies.
